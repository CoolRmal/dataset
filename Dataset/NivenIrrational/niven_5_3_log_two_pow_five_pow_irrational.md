## Statement

**§5.3, Example 3.** Let $c$ and $d$ be two different non-negative integers. Prove that $\log(2^c5^d)$ is irrational.

**Notation.** All logarithms are to base $10$: $\log y = k$ means $10^k = y$. The proofs are based on the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.
