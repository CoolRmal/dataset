# Criteria: niven_5_5_constructible_degree_is_power_of_two

**Statement:** [niven_5_5_constructible_degree_is_power_of_two.md](niven_5_5_constructible_degree_is_power_of_two.md) · **Lean:** [niven_5_5_constructible_degree_is_power_of_two.lean](niven_5_5_constructible_degree_is_power_of_two.lean)

Two conclusions: constructible numbers are algebraic, and their degree is a power of `2`. The degree is the dimension of `ℚ(x)` over `ℚ`, and the exponent `k` may depend on `x` — hoisting `∃ k` outside `∀ x` would claim a uniform degree bound. `IsConstructible` is an inductive closure of `ℚ` under `+`, `-`, `*`, `⁻¹` and square roots of non-negative constructed reals. Two things must be checked in any candidate: that the square-root clause is guarded by non-negativity (mathlib's `Real.sqrt` of a negative number is the junk value `0`, so an unguarded clause would make every real constructible via `√(-1) = 0`… harmlessly, but it would also admit `√x` for `x < 0` as a *new* constructible number equal to `0`), and that the base case is all of `ℚ` rather than just `1`.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
