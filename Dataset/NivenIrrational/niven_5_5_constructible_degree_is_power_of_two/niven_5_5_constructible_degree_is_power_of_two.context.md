## Notation

**Constructible** lengths are those reachable from $\mathbb{Q}$ by the field operations and square roots of **non-negative** constructed quantities. **Degree** is the degree of the minimal polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$.

Degree $1$ is allowed, since every rational is constructible.
