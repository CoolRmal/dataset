## Statement

**8.19. Theorem.** For a compact metric space $X$,

$$\mathcal{H}^s(X) = \sup\{\mathcal{H}^s(C) : C \subset X \text{ is compact with } \mathcal{H}^s(C) < \infty\}.$$
