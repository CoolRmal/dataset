# Criteria: mattila_10_10_plane_sections

**Statement:** [mattila_10_10_plane_sections.md](mattila_10_10_plane_sections.md) · **Lean:** [mattila_10_10_plane_sections.lean](mattila_10_10_plane_sections.lean)

A faithful formalization must carry two clauses with *different* quantifier patterns over the Grassmannian: a finiteness statement holding for **all** $W \in G(n,n-m)$ and $\mathcal H^m$-a.e. translate $a \in W^{\perp}$, and a positivity statement holding for $\gamma_{n,n-m}$-**a.e.** $W$, asserting that a set of translates of *positive* $\mathcal H^m$ measure gives sections of the expected dimension $t-m$. The two hazards are the parametrization of the family of parallel planes $W_a$ (indexed by $a \in W^{\perp}$, with the Hausdorff measure taken inside that $m$-dimensional subspace) and whether the invariant measure $\gamma_{n,n-m}$ is actually determined by the hypotheses imposed on it.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Semantic closeness / a.e. quantifier | "For $\gamma_{n,n-m}$ almost all $W$" needs $\gamma_{n,n-m}$ to be determined. Orthogonal invariance plus total mass $1$ does characterize it — but only once the σ-algebra on $G(n,n-m)$ is the Borel structure of its natural compact topology. | ❗ `[MeasurableSpace (Grassmannian n (n - m))]` is an unconstrained instance argument, so the statement is asserted for every measurable structure. With `⊥`, `Measure.dirac W₀` satisfies `IsInvariantGrassmannianMeasure` and `∀ᵐ W ∂γ` degenerates to `∀ W` (both verified in Lean). Clause (2) then fails for, e.g., $A = C \times [0,1] \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ with $\dim C = t-1$ and $W$ the vertical direction. The scaffolding needs the Borel structure pinned down. |
| 2 | Semantic closeness / quantifier order | The two clauses must keep their distinct patterns: clause (1) is `∀ W, ∀ᵐ a`, clause (2) is `∀ᵐ W, (positive-measure set of a)`. Merging them, or making clause (2) an "a.e. $a$" statement, is wrong: only a positive-measure set of translates is claimed, never almost all. | ✅ `(∀ W, ∀ᵐ a ∂μH[(m:ℝ)], μH[t-m] (slice W a) < ∞) ∧ (∀ᵐ W ∂γ, 0 < μH[(m:ℝ)] {a \| dimH (slice W a) = …})`. ❗ Predicted error: upgrading clause (2) to "for $\mathcal H^m$ a.e. $a$". |
| 3 | Faithful encoding | $W_a$ is the affine plane $W + a$, indexed by $a \in W^{\perp}$; the section is $A \cap W_a$. In Lean: `a : ↥W.1ᗮ` coerced into $\mathbb{R}^n$, and `A ∩ {x \| x - (a : EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)) ∈ W.1}`. | ✅ Exactly this, via the local `slice` abbreviation. ❗ Predicted error: indexing the family by arbitrary $a \in \mathbb{R}^n$ (which double-counts each plane along $W$ and destroys the $\mathcal H^m$-measure statements), or by points of $W$ rather than $W^{\perp}$. |
| 4 | Faithful encoding / measures on subspaces | $\dim W = n - m$, so $W^{\perp}$ is $m$-dimensional and $\mathcal H^m$ on $W^{\perp}$ is the natural measure there. Both clauses use it only through "a.e." and "measure $> 0$", so the constant relating $\mathcal H^m$ to Lebesgue measure on $W^{\perp}$ is irrelevant. | ✅ `(μH[(m:ℝ)] : Measure ↥W.1ᗮ)` for the translates; `(μH[t-m] : Measure (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin n)))` for the section, computed in the ambient space (equal to the intrinsic value since $W_a \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$ is isometric). |
| 5 | Junk values | `dimH` is `ℝ≥0∞`-valued, so the target dimension must be coerced correctly: `ENNReal.ofReal (t - m)` with `t - m > 0` guaranteed by the hypothesis. | ✅ `dimH (slice W a) = ENNReal.ofReal (t - m)`. ❗ Predicted error: comparing `dimH` against a real, or using an `ℝ≥0∞` subtraction `(t : ℝ≥0∞) - m` where truncation could bite. |
| 6 | Junk values | `Grassmannian n (n - m)` uses truncated ℕ subtraction; if `m ≥ n` this silently becomes `Grassmannian n 0`, the one-point Grassmannian, and the theorem degenerates. | ✅ Guarded: `hmt : (m : ℝ) < t ∧ t < n` forces `m < n`, so `n - m` is the true difference. |
| 7 | Hypothesis completeness | All of $m < t$, $t < n$, `MeasurableSet A` (Borel, as in the text), $\mathcal H^t(A) < \infty$ and $\mathcal H^t(A) > 0$ are needed — finiteness for clause (1), positivity for clause (2). | ✅ All five present (`hmt`, `hA`, `hAfinite`, `hApos`). ⚠️ `hmt` is packaged as a conjunction; two separate hypotheses would be more idiomatic. |
| 8 | Conclusion completeness | Both clauses must be present. Clause (1) alone is the routine Fubini-type bound; clause (2) — the existence of a positive-measure set of sections of full dimension for a.e. direction — is the substance of 10.10. | ✅ Both, as a conjunction. ❗ Predicted error: formalizing only clause (1), or replacing `0 < μH[m] {…}` by `∃ a, dimH (slice W a) = t - m`. |
