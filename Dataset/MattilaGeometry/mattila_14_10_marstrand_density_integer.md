## Statement

**14.10. Theorem.** Let $s$ be a positive number. Suppose that there exists a Radon measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb{R}^n$ such that the density $\Theta^s(\mu, a)$ exists and is positive and finite in a set of positive $\mu$ measure. Then $s$ is an integer.
