# Criteria: kong_3_2_3_characteristic_multiplier_stability

**Statement:** [kong_3_2_3_characteristic_multiplier_stability.md](kong_3_2_3_characteristic_multiplier_stability.md) · **Lean:** [kong_3_2_3_characteristic_multiplier_stability.lean](kong_3_2_3_characteristic_multiplier_stability.lean)

A faithful formalization must state three equivalences relating the three stability regimes of the $\omega$-periodic linear system to the moduli of the characteristic multipliers, with the borderline case $\lvert\mu_i\rvert = 1$ controlled by the semisimplicity of $\mu_i$ in the transition matrix $V$. Almost all of the content sits in the auxiliary definitions: the multipliers must be the eigenvalues of $V$ *with multiplicity* (not a set), "in the diagonal Jordan block" must be the correct algebraic condition, and the stability notions must have $\delta$ chosen before $t_0$ — the quantifier order is what distinguishes uniform stability from plain Lyapunov stability.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Faithful encoding | "The characteristic multipliers $\mu_1,\dots,\mu_n$" is an $n$-tuple of eigenvalues *with algebraic multiplicity*, since (a) and (b) quantify over $i = 1,\dots,n$. | ✅ `CharacteristicMultipliers V μ := Matrix.charpoly V = ∏ i, (Polynomial.X - Polynomial.C (μ i))`, which pins `μ : Fin n → ℂ` to the multiset of roots of the monic `charpoly = det (X • 1 - V)` (cf. `Matrix.charpoly_diagonal`). ❗ Trap: `∀ i, μ i ∈ spectrum ℂ V` (loses multiplicity and does not force *all* eigenvalues to be listed). |
| 2 | Faithful encoding | The transition matrix must come from *some* fundamental matrix solution of the periodic equation, complexified, and the statement must be independent of that choice. | ✅ `IsPeriodTransitionMatrix ω A V := ∃ X, FundamentalMatrixSolution univ A X ∧ V = (X ω * (X 0)⁻¹).map (algebraMap ℝ ℂ)`; well-posed since any two fundamental matrices differ by a constant right factor `X̃ = X M`, giving `X̃ ω * (X̃ 0)⁻¹ = X ω * (X 0)⁻¹`. ✅ Kong's $V$ (from $X(t+\omega)=X(t)V$, i.e. $X^{-1}(0)X(\omega)$) is conjugate to this one, so both the multipliers and the Jordan condition are unaffected. |
| 3 | Faithful encoding | "$\mu_i$ is in the diagonal Jordan block of $V$" means every Jordan block of $V$ for $\mu_i$ is $1\times 1$, i.e. $\ker(V-\mu_i)^2 = \ker(V-\mu_i)$. | ✅ `InDiagonalJordanBlock V μ := ∀ v, (V - μ • 1) *ᵥ ((V - μ • 1) *ᵥ v) = 0 → (V - μ • 1) *ᵥ v = 0` — exactly that. ❗ Traps: `V.IsDiag` (asks the whole matrix to be diagonal), or "$\mu_i$ is a simple root of the characteristic polynomial" (too strong: a repeated *semisimple* multiplier of modulus 1 is allowed). |
| 4 | Semantic closeness | Uniform stability is $\forall \varepsilon > 0\ \exists \delta > 0\ \forall t_0\ \forall$ solutions: $\delta$ must not depend on $t_0$. Swapping to $\forall t_0\ \exists\delta$ gives plain stability, a different (here equivalent, but unfaithfully stated) notion. | ✅ `UniformlyStableZeroSolution F := ∀ ε > 0, ∃ δ > 0, ∀ t₀ x, IsTrajectory F x → ‖x t₀‖ < δ → ∀ t, t₀ ≤ t → ‖x t‖ < ε`, with `δ` bound before `t₀`. ✅ `∀ t ≥ t₀` (forward time only) is correct. |
| 5 | Semantic closeness | Asymptotic stability in Kong's sense (b) is uniform stability **plus** a $t_0$-independent attraction radius; it is not merely $x(t) \to 0$. | ✅ `AsymptoticallyStableZeroSolution F := UniformlyStableZeroSolution F ∧ ∃ δ > 0, ∀ t₀ x, IsTrajectory F x → ‖x t₀‖ < δ → Tendsto x atTop (𝓝 0)`. ❗ Trap: dropping the uniform-stability conjunct, or letting `δ` depend on `t₀`. |
| 6 | Conclusion completeness | All three equivalences (a), (b), (c) must appear, each as an `↔`, with the borderline clause of (a) stated as an implication `‖μ i‖ = 1 → InDiagonalJordanBlock V (μ i)` and its negation correctly De Morgan-ed in (c). | ✅ Three conjoined `↔`s; (c) reads `∃ i, 1 < ‖μ i‖ ∨ (‖μ i‖ = 1 ∧ ¬InDiagonalJordanBlock V (μ i))`. ⚠️ Since `UnstableLinearEquation A := ¬UniformlyStableLinearEquation A`, part (c) is literally the contrapositive of part (a) and carries no independent content; it is faithful to the text's list but logically redundant. Note also that Kong's "unstable" negates *stability*, whereas the Lean negates *uniform* stability (equivalent for linear periodic systems). |
| 7 | Hypothesis completeness | Kong's (H-p) assumes $A$ continuous and $\omega$-periodic. The Lean assumes only `hω : 0 < ω` and `PeriodicLinearEquation ω A`. | ⚠️ Continuity of `A` is omitted, so the statement is formally stronger than Kong's — but harmlessly: `hV` supplies a fundamental matrix `X`, and for any trajectory `y` the function `X⁻¹ y` has derivative `0` everywhere, so the solution set is exactly `{X(t)c}` regardless of the regularity of `A`; `X` and `X⁻¹` are continuous hence bounded on `[0, ω]`, which is all the proof needs. |
| 8 | Faithful encoding | The three stability predicates must be applied to the *linear* field `fun t x ↦ A t *ᵥ x`, and the trajectories quantified over must be genuine solutions (`HasDerivAt`, not `deriv`). | ✅ `UniformlyStableLinearEquation A := UniformlyStableZeroSolution fun t x ↦ A t *ᵥ x`, and `IsTrajectory F x := ∀ t, HasDerivAt x (F t (x t)) t`. ✅ Requiring trajectories to be defined on all of `ℝ` is exactly right for linear systems, where every maximal solution is global. |
