## Notation

The **transition matrix** is $V = X(\omega)X^{-1}(0)$ and the **characteristic multipliers** are its eigenvalues listed with algebraic multiplicity. "In the diagonal Jordan block" means the eigenvalue is semisimple — all its Jordan blocks are $1\times1$. "Uniformly" stable means the radius does not depend on the initial time.
