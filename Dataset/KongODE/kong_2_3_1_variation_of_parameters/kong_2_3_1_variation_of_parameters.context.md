## Notation

A **fundamental matrix solution** solves $X'=A(t)X$ **and** is nonsingular at every $t$. "The general solution is …" is an equivalence characterising the whole solution set, with $c$ chosen per solution. The integral is oriented, so $t$ may lie on either side of $t_0$.
