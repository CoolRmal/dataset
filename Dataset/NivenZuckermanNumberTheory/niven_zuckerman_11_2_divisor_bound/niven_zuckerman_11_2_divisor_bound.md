## Statement

**Lemma 11.2.** The function $\tau(n)$, representing the number of positive divisors of $n$, satisfies the inequality $\tau(n) \le 2\sqrt{n}$ for $n \ge 1$.

**Notation.** $\tau(n)$ is the number of positive divisors of $n$.
