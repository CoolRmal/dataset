## Notation

The density here is the **asymptotic** (natural) density of Definition 11.1, $\lim_{n\to\infty}A(n)/n$ where $A(n)$ counts the elements of $A$ in $1 \le a \le n$ — not the Schnirelmann density of Definition 11.2, which is an infimum. Asserting the density equals $6/\pi^2$ asserts that the limit exists.
