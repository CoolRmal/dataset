## Notation

$\mu$ is the Möbius function: $\mu(1)=1$, $\mu(n)=(-1)^k$ for a product of $k$ distinct primes, and $\mu(n)=0$ when a square $>1$ divides $n$. Both series run over $n \ge 1$, and what is asserted is that the **product of the two sums** is $1$.
