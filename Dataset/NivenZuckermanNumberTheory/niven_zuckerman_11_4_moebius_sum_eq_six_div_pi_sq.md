## Statement

**Corollary 11.4.** We have

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\mu(n)}{n^2} = \frac{6}{\pi^2}.$$

**Notation.** $\mu$ is the Möbius function; the proof uses the classical evaluation $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n^2 = \pi^2/6$.
