## Statement

**Theorem 11.3.** We have

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\mu(n)}{n^2}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^2} = 1.$$

**Notation.** $\mu$ is the Möbius function.
