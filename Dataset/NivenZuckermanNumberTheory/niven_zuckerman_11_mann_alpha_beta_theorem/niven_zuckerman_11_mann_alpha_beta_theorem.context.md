## Notation

The density here is the **Schnirelmann** density of Definition 11.2, $d(A) = \inf_{n\ge1}A(n)/n$ — an infimum over all $n \ge 1$, not the asymptotic density of Definition 11.1. It is very sensitive to small $n$: if $1 \notin A$ then $d(A) = 0$.
