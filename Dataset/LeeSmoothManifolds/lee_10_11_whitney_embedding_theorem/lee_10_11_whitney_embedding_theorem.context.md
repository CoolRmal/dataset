## Notation

A **smooth embedding** is an immersion *and* a topological embedding; "smooth + topological embedding" is strictly weaker ($t \mapsto t^3$). **Proper** means preimages of compact sets are compact, which is what forces the image to be closed. Lee's "smooth $n$-manifold" carries Hausdorff and second countability.
