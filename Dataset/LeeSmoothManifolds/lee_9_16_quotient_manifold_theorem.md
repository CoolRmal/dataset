## Statement

**Theorem 9.16 (Quotient Manifold Theorem).** Suppose a Lie group $G$ acts smoothly, freely, and properly on a smooth manifold $M$. Then the orbit space $M/G$ is a topological manifold of dimension equal to $\dim M - \dim G$, and has a unique smooth structure with the property that the quotient map $\pi \colon M \to M/G$ is a smooth submersion.
