## Notation

A **tubular neighbourhood** is a neighbourhood of $M$ that is the diffeomorphic image of a disk bundle $\{(x,v) : v \perp T_xM,\ |v| < \delta(x)\}$ under $(x,v)\mapsto x+v$. The radius $\delta$ is a positive continuous function on $M$, not a constant.
