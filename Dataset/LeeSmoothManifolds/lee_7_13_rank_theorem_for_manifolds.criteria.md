# Criteria: lee_7_13_rank_theorem_for_manifolds

**Statement:** [lee_7_13_rank_theorem_for_manifolds.md](lee_7_13_rank_theorem_for_manifolds.md) · **Lean:** [lee_7_13_rank_theorem_for_manifolds.lean](lee_7_13_rank_theorem_for_manifolds.lean)

A faithful formalization needs smooth manifolds of *fixed* dimensions $m$ and $n$, a smooth $F$ whose differential $dF_p$ has rank exactly $k$ at **every** point, and, at each $p$, two genuinely *smooth* coordinate charts — members of the maximal $C^\infty$ atlas, not arbitrary homeomorphisms — in which $F$ reads $(x^1,\dots,x^m) \mapsto (x^1,\dots,x^k,0,\dots,0)$. The main junk-value risk is `mfderiv`, which Mathlib defines to be the zero map when `F` is not `MDifferentiableAt` the point: without the smoothness hypothesis the constant-rank condition degenerates to `k = 0`. A secondary point is whether the typeclass stack really says "smooth manifold" in Lee's sense, which includes Hausdorff and second countable.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |

| 1 | Junk values | `ConstantRank F k` is `∀ p, Module.finrank ℝ (LinearMap.range (mfderiv 𝓘(ℝ, Fin m → ℝ) 𝓘(ℝ, Fin n → ℝ) F p).toLinearMap) = k`. `mfderiv` is `0` at points where `F` is not `MDifferentiableAt`, so *on its own* this predicate is satisfied with `k = 0` by any sufficiently pathological `F`. | ✅ `hF : ContMDiff 𝓘(ℝ, Fin m → ℝ) 𝓘(ℝ, Fin n → ℝ) ∞ F` is present and makes `mfderiv` the honest differential. ❗ Predicted error: a candidate that states the constant-rank hypothesis but weakens `hF` to `Continuous F`, or drops it entirely because "constant rank already implies differentiability" — it does not, in Lean. |
| 2 | Faithful encoding | "Smooth coordinates centered at $p$" must be charts of the *smooth* structure. `φ ∈ IsManifold.maximalAtlas 𝓘(ℝ, Fin m → ℝ) ∞ M` (and the mirror for `ψ`) is exactly this. | ✅ Membership in the maximal $C^\infty$ atlas is asserted for both charts. ❗ Predicted error: producing a bare `OpenPartialHomeomorph` (a topological chart, so the normal form would say nothing about the smooth structure), or using the fixed `chartAt` of the `ChartedSpace` instance, which is a different and unprovable claim. |
| 3 | Conclusion completeness | The normal form and everything needed for it to typecheck as a statement about $\psi \circ F \circ \varphi^{-1}$ on all of $\varphi(U)$: `p ∈ φ.source`, `F p ∈ ψ.source`, `MapsTo F φ.source ψ.source`, and `∀ x ∈ φ.target, ψ (F (φ.symm x)) = fun i ↦ if h : i.1 < k ∧ i.1 < m then x ⟨i.1, h.2⟩ else 0`. | ✅ All present; the formula copies the first $k$ coordinates and zeroes the remaining $n-k$, matching $(x^1,\dots,x^k,0,\dots,0)$. | 5 | Mathlib conventions / typeclass stack | Lee's "smooth manifold of dimension $m$" = second countable, Hausdorff, locally Euclidean with a $C^\infty$ atlas. `[ChartedSpace (Fin m → ℝ) M]` + `[IsManifold 𝓘(ℝ, Fin m → ℝ) ∞ M]` gives locally Euclidean of dimension $m$, boundaryless, with $C^\infty$ transitions — but Mathlib carries Hausdorffness and second countability as the separate typeclasses `[T2Space M]`, `[SecondCountableTopology M]`, which are absent here. | ⚠️ Harmless *for this theorem*: the rank theorem is purely local, so the Lean version is strictly more general and still true. | 6 | Hypothesis completeness | `hk : k ≤ m ∧ k ≤ n` is redundant — the rank of a map $T_pM \to T_{F(p)}N$ is at most $\min(m,n)$, and if `M` is empty both `hrank` and the conclusion are vacuous. | ⚠️ Noise rather than error; a candidate omitting it is at least as faithful. |
| 7 | Mathlib conventions | The model space is `Fin m → ℝ` (the sup-norm product) rather than Mathlib's usual `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin m)`. The two carry the same smooth structure and the same `ModelWithCorners`-induced notion of `ContMDiff`, so nothing in this statement changes; but it is off-convention, and it *would* matter in a statement mentioning distances or norms. | ✅ / ⚠️ Acceptable here; a candidate using `EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin m)` should be regarded as equally good or better. |
