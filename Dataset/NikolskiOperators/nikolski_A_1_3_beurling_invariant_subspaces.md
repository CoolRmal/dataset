## Statement

**1.3.2. Theorem (A. Beurling, H. Helson).** Let $E \subset L^2$, $zE \subsetneq E$. Then there exists a measurable function $\Theta$, unique up to a multiplicative constant of modulus $1$, such that $|\Theta| = 1$ a.e. on $\mathbb{T}$ and

$$E = \Theta H^2.$$
