## Statement

**1.3.2. Theorem (Z. Nehari, 1957).** If $H : H^2 \to H^2_-$ is a bounded Hankel operator, then there exists $\varphi \in L^\infty$ such that $H = H_\varphi$ and

$$\|H_\varphi\| = \|\varphi\|_\infty = \operatorname{dist}(\varphi, H^\infty).$$
