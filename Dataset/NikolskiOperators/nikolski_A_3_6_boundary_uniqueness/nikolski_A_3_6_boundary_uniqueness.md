## Statement

**3.6.1. Corollary.** If $g \in H^1$, $g \ne 0$, then $\log|g| \in L^1(\mathbb{T})$. In particular, if $g \in H^1$ and

$$m\{t \in \mathbb{T} : g(t) = 0\} > 0,$$

then $g = 0$.
