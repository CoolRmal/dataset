## Notation

A **Hankel** matrix has entries depending on $i+j$ (Toeplitz: $i-j$). A symbol is unique only modulo $H^\infty$; the theorem picks one whose $L^\infty$ norm equals $\operatorname{dist}(\varphi,H^\infty)$, and all three quantities concern that same $\varphi$.
