## Notation

$f$ is **inner** when $|f|=1$ a.e. on $\mathbb{T}$, **outer** when $E_f = H^2$, where $E_f$ is the smallest closed shift-invariant subspace containing $f$. "$f \ne 0$" means $f$ is not the zero element, not that $f$ has no zeros. Uniqueness uses a single constant shared by the two factors.
