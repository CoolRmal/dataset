# Criteria: bogachev_4_5_9_de_la_vallee_poussin

**Statement:** [bogachev_4_5_9_de_la_vallee_poussin.md](bogachev_4_5_9_de_la_vallee_poussin.md) · **Lean:** [bogachev_4_5_9_de_la_vallee_poussin.lean](bogachev_4_5_9_de_la_vallee_poussin.lean)

A faithful formalization must state the full equivalence (uniform integrability ⟺ existence of a superlinear $G$ with uniformly bounded $\int G(|f|)\,d\mu$) *and* the refinement that $G$ can additionally be chosen convex, over a family of functions that is assumed integrable, with respect to a finite measure. The integral $\int G(|f|)\,d\mu$ may fail to be finite, so the encoding of that integral is where junk values hide.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
