# Criteria: bogachev_4_6_3_nikodym_vitali_hahn_saks

**Statement:** [bogachev_4_6_3_nikodym_vitali_hahn_saks.md](bogachev_4_6_3_nikodym_vitali_hahn_saks.md) · **Lean:** [bogachev_4_6_3_nikodym_vitali_hahn_saks.lean](bogachev_4_6_3_nikodym_vitali_hahn_saks.lean)

A four-part conclusion under a single hypothesis (setwise convergence of a sequence of real measures): (i) the setwise limit is again a countably additive real measure; (ii) a uniform modulus $|\mu_n(A)| \le \alpha(\nu(A))$ with $\nu$ finite nonnegative and $\alpha$ bounded, nondecreasing, vanishing at $0^+$; (iii) uniform boundedness in variation and uniform countable additivity; (iv) uniformity of absolute continuity against any dominating finite measure. Every clause is a known dropping point; “real measure” must mean signed measure.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| 1 | Faithful encoding | “Measures in $\mathcal{M}(X,\mathcal{A})$” are finite *signed* measures.
