# Criteria: bogachev_9_1_9_radon_preimage_from_compact_approximation

**Statement:** [bogachev_9_1_9_radon_preimage_from_compact_approximation.md](bogachev_9_1_9_radon_preimage_from_compact_approximation.md) · **Lean:** [bogachev_9_1_9_radon_preimage_from_compact_approximation.lean](bogachev_9_1_9_radon_preimage_from_compact_approximation.lean)

The central difficulty: $f$ is **not** assumed measurable — it is only continuous on the compacts $K_n$ — so the conclusion “$\mu \circ f^{-1} = \nu$” cannot be encoded as `Measure.map`/a pushforward along a measurable map. The image measure must be expressed through $|\mu|$-completion-measurability of preimages: for each Borel $A \subseteq Y$ there is a Borel $B \subseteq X$ agreeing with $f^{-1}(A)$ up to a $|\mu|$-null set, with $\mu(B) = \nu(A)$. Radon-ness of a finite signed measure is inner regularity of its total variation.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
