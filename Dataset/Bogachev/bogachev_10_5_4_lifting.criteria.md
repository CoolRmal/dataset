# Criteria: bogachev_10_5_4_lifting

**Statement:** [bogachev_10_5_4_lifting.md](bogachev_10_5_4_lifting.md) · **Lean:** [bogachev_10_5_4_lifting.lean](bogachev_10_5_4_lifting.lean)

The theorem asserts existence of a lifting: a simultaneous, *pointwise-everywhere* coherent choice of representatives for a.e.-equivalence classes of bounded measurable functions, which is linear, multiplicative, unital, and respects a.e. equality. The formalization's entire fidelity rests on the everywhere/a.e. distinction: several of the lifting axioms are exact equalities of functions, and stating any of them only up to a.e. equality trivializes the theorem (the identity map would qualify).

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Faithful encoding | The lifting must act on genuine functions `(X → ℝ) → (X → ℝ)`, not on the quotient `Lp ∞ μ` into itself: the content is the choice of pointwise representatives, so the codomain must be functions and the axioms pointwise. A candidate typed `Lp ∞ μ → Lp ∞ μ` cannot even express conditions (ii)–(v). | ✅ `toFun : (X → ℝ) → X → ℝ`. ❗ Trap: quotient-to-quotient maps. |
| 2 | Faithful encoding | Axioms (ii)–(v) are **exact** equalities (`toFun f = toFun g`, `toFun (f+g) = toFun f + toFun g`, …); only (i) `L(f) = f` is a.e. (`=ᵐ[μ]`). Swapping any exact equality for `=ᵐ[μ]` destroys the theorem (identity would be a lifting). | ✅ `congr_ae`, `map_ae_one`, `map_add`, `map_smul`, `map_mul` are exact; `representative` is `=ᵐ[μ]`. |
| 3 | Domain encoding | The book's $\mathcal{L}^\infty_{\mathcal{A}}$ is the set of *bounded measurable* functions. With a total function type, each axiom must be guarded by boundedness + measurability of its arguments (`Measurable f ∧ ∃ C, ∀ x, |f x| ≤ C`), and the lifting must map this class into itself (`maps_bounded_measurable`). Unguarded axioms over all of `X → ℝ` would state a much stronger (false) theorem. | ✅ Every field is guarded; closure of the class under `L` is a field. ⚠️ A subtype `{f : X → ℝ // Measurable f ∧ …}` would avoid repeating guards; the guarded total-function form is acceptable and keeps the statement readable. |
| 4 | Hypothesis completeness | Both hypotheses on `μ` are essential: completeness (`μ.IsComplete`) — the theorem is proved for complete measures and the standard construction uses it — and probability/finiteness (`IsProbabilityMeasure μ`). Dropping `IsComplete` is the most likely omission. | ✅ `[IsProbabilityMeasure μ] [μ.IsComplete]`. |
| 5 | Conclusion completeness | All five lifting axioms of Definition 10.5.1 must be present: representative (i), a.e.-congruence (ii), unitality (iii), linearity (iv) — both additivity and scalar homogeneity — and multiplicativity (v). Models most often drop (iii) or merge (ii) into (i). | ✅ Seven structure fields cover (i)–(v) (linearity split into `map_add`/`map_smul`). |
| 6 | Semantic closeness | Existence is stated as `Nonempty (LInfinityLifting μ)` with the lifting packaged as a structure — semantically close to “there exists a lifting” and reusable. An unfolded `∃ L : (X → ℝ) → X → ℝ, …` seven-way conjunction would be equivalent but unwieldy; either is acceptable. | ✅ `Nonempty` of a named structure. |
| 7 | Mathlib conventions | Scalars ℝ for “$\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}^1$”, scalar action via `c • f`, pointwise algebra via the function instances (`f + g`, `f * g`, `(1 : X → ℝ)`), a.e. equality via `=ᵐ[μ]`. | ✅ Conventions followed. |
