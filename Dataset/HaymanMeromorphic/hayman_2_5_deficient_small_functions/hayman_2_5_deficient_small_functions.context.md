## Notation

$N(r,a)$ counts $a$-points with multiplicity and $\bar N(r,a)$ without; the theorems turn on which appears. $T(r,f)$ is the characteristic. $\delta$ and $\theta$ use lower limits, $\Theta$ an upper one. **Admissible** in the plane means $T(r,f)\to\infty$. $S(r,f)$ is any error term of size $o\{T(r,f)\}$.

The $a_\nu$ are **functions**, not constants, and $\bar N(r,1/(f-a_\nu))$ counts the **zeros** of $f-a_\nu$ without multiplicity.
