## Notation

An *integral function* is an **entire** one, and *transcendental* means not a polynomial. **Exact order $n$** means $f_n(z)=z$ **and** $f_m(z)\ne z$ for all $1\le m<n$; without the exactness clause the statement collapses, since a fix-point of order $1$ is one of every order.
