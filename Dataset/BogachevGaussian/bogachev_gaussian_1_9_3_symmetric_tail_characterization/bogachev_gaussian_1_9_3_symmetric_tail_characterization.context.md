## Notation

**Symmetric** means invariant under $x\mapsto-x$, strictly stronger than mean zero. The $\sqrt2$ is the variance normalisation; without it the hypothesis is unsatisfiable for non-degenerate laws. "Gaussian" includes the degenerate case $\sigma=0$, so $\xi\equiv0$ qualifies.
