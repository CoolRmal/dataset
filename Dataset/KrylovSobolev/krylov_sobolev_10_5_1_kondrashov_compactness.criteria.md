# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_10_5_1_kondrashov_compactness

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_10_5_1_kondrashov_compactness.md](krylov_sobolev_10_5_1_kondrashov_compactness.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_10_5_1_kondrashov_compactness.lean](krylov_sobolev_10_5_1_kondrashov_compactness.lean)

A faithful formalization must say: bounded in $W_p^k(\Omega)$ $\Rightarrow$ **precompact** in $W_q^m(\Omega)$, under the **strict** inequality $k - d/p > m - d/q$ on a **bounded** domain of class $C^k$. Each of the three emphasised words is a separate failure mode: with equality one only gets a continuous embedding (Theorem 10.4.4) and not compactness; on an unbounded domain the embedding is never compact (translate a fixed bump to infinity); and without boundary regularity the embedding can fail altogether (outward-cusp and "rooms and passages" domains). "Precompact" means every sequence has a subsequence converging in the $W_q^m$ norm to *some* element of $W_q^m(\Omega)$ — the limit need not lie in $U$.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
