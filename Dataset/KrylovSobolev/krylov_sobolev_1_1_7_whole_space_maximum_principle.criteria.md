# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_1_1_7_whole_space_maximum_principle

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_1_1_7_whole_space_maximum_principle.md](krylov_sobolev_1_1_7_whole_space_maximum_principle.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_1_1_7_whole_space_maximum_principle.lean](krylov_sobolev_1_1_7_whole_space_maximum_principle.lean)

A faithful formalization must carry both assertions of the lemma — the one-sided one ($u$ bounded *from above*, $\Delta u - \lambda u \ge 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $u \le 0$) and the Liouville consequence ($u$ bounded, $\Delta u - \lambda u = 0$ $\Rightarrow$ $u \equiv 0$) — with $\lambda > 0$ strictly. The strictness is the whole point: at $\lambda = 0$ the first assertion is false ($u \equiv 1$ is bounded above with $\Delta u = 0$), and Remark 1.1.9 replaces the conclusion by "$u$ is a constant". No decay of $u$ at infinity may be assumed; the barrier $\cosh(\varepsilon\|x\|)$ in the proof exists precisely to avoid it.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| 6 | Hypothesis completeness / no decay | Nothing may be assumed about the behaviour of $u$ at infinity beyond one-sided boundedness — no $u \to 0$, no `MemLp`, no compact support.
