# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_1_1_1_energy_identity

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_1_1_1_energy_identity.md](krylov_sobolev_1_1_1_energy_identity.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_1_1_1_energy_identity.lean](krylov_sobolev_1_1_1_energy_identity.lean)

A faithful formalization must state an *equality* of four squared $\mathcal{L}_2(\mathbb{R}^d)$ quantities with the exact coefficients $\lambda^2$, $2\lambda$, $1$ — this is Parseval applied to $|\hat f|^2 = (\lambda + |\xi|^2)^2|\hat u|^2$, so every constant is forced and no constant $N$ appears. The three hazards are the class $C_0^2$ (both differentiability *and* compact support are load-bearing: without compact support none of the four integrals need converge), the range of the double sum (all $d^2$ ordered pairs $(j,k)$, not the $\binom{d+1}{2}$ unordered ones), and the fact that the classical derivatives are assembled from `fderiv`, which silently returns `0` off the differentiability locus.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| 1 | Hypothesis completeness | $u \in C_0^2$ carries two conditions: twice continuous differentiability **and** compact support. Dropping compact support destroys the statement — for $u \equiv 1$ (with $f = \lambda$) every term is $\infty$, and in Lean each divergent Bochner integral silently becomes `0`, turning (2) into the false identity $0 = 0$ only by accident of the junk convention. | ✅ `hu : ContDiff ℝ 2 u` and `hsupp : HasCompactSupport u` are both present. ❗ Predicted error: assuming only `ContDiff ℝ 2 u`, or replacing compact support by `MemLp u 2`, which does not make $u_{xx} \in \mathcal{L}_2$ available. |
| 2 | Conclusion completeness / exact constants | The identity has coefficients $\lambda^2$, $2\lambda$, $1$ and is an equality. Weakening `=` to `≤` or `≥`, or inserting an unspecified constant $N$, loses the entire content: the point of Lemma 1.1.1 is that the three left-hand terms are controlled *with sharp constants*, which is what makes the $\mathcal{L}_2$ theory of $\lambda u - \Delta u = f$ elementary. | ✅ `lam ^ 2 * (∫ x, u x ^ 2) + 2 * lam * (∑ j, ∫ …) + (∑ j, ∑ k, ∫ …) = ∫ x, f x ^ 2`. ❗ Predicted error: writing `≤`, or dropping the middle gradient term (which is the only one carrying the cross-term $2\lambda$). |
