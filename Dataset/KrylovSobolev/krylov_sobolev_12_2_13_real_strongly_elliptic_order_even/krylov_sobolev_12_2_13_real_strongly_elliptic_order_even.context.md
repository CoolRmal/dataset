## Notation

**Strong ellipticity** is *two* conditions: the principal symbol $\sum_{|\alpha|=m}a^\alpha\xi^\alpha$ does not vanish off the origin, **and** the characteristic polynomial $\sigma(\xi)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le m}a^\alpha i^{|\alpha|}\xi^\alpha$ does not vanish anywhere. The powers of $i$ are not decoration. Reality is assumed of *every* coefficient.
