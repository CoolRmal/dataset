# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_10_3_2_gagliardo_nirenberg

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_10_3_2_gagliardo_nirenberg.md](krylov_sobolev_10_3_2_gagliardo_nirenberg.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_10_3_2_gagliardo_nirenberg.lean](krylov_sobolev_10_3_2_gagliardo_nirenberg.lean)

A faithful formalization must state both halves — the membership $u \in \mathcal{L}_{d/(d-1)}(\Omega)$ and the bound — and must keep the bound in its **sharp product form** $\prod_{j=1}^d\|D_ju\|_{\mathcal{L}_1}^{1/d}$ with constant exactly $1$. Replacing the geometric mean by $N\|u_x\|_{\mathcal{L}_1}$ is the usual textbook weakening and loses the whole point of the induction on $d$. The delicate encoding question is the exponent $d/(d-1)$: it must be computed in a way that gives $\infty$ at $d = 1$ (Krylov's base case) rather than a truncated natural-number or real division.
