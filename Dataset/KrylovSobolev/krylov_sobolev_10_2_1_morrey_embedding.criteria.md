# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_10_2_1_morrey_embedding

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_10_2_1_morrey_embedding.md](krylov_sobolev_10_2_1_morrey_embedding.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_10_2_1_morrey_embedding.lean](krylov_sobolev_10_2_1_morrey_embedding.lean)

A faithful formalization must carry three things: the Campanato-type hypothesis $\fint_B\lvert u_x\rvert\,dx \le Mr_B^{\alpha-1}$ over **all** balls $B \subset \Omega$; the two conclusions (2) and (3); and the "in particular" clause specialising to $p > d$ with $\alpha = 1-d/p$ and $M = \|u_x\|_{\mathcal{L}_p(\Omega)}$. The decisive subtlety is that (2) as printed is false for a general $\mathcal{L}_1$ representative of $u$ — the proof opens with "we are going to prove that there exists a modification of $u$ satisfying (2) and (3)", so the existential over an a.e.-equal $v$ is part of the statement, not an artefact of the encoding.
