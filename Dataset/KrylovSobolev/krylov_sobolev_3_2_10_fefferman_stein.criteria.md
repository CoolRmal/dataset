# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_3_2_10_fefferman_stein

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_3_2_10_fefferman_stein.md](krylov_sobolev_3_2_10_fefferman_stein.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_3_2_10_fefferman_stein.lean](krylov_sobolev_3_2_10_fefferman_stein.lean)

A faithful formalization must state the *hard* direction — the $\mathcal{L}_p$ norm of $f$ dominated by that of its sharp function $f^{\#}$ — with the explicit constant $(2q)^pN_0^{p-1}$, on a complete $\sigma$-finite measure space of **infinite** total mass equipped with a filtration of partitions whose regularity constant is that same $N_0$. The reverse inequality $\|f^{\#}\|_p \le 2\|\mathcal{M}f\|_p \le 2q\|f\|_p$ is elementary and is *not* this theorem. Every axiom of Definition 3.1.1 is load-bearing: with the stationary filtration $\mathbb{C}_n = \{[k,k+1) : k \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ on $\mathbb{R}$ (nested, regular with $N_0 = 1$, cells of finite measure, but $\inf_C\lvert C\rvert\not\to\infty$) the indicator of $[0,1)$ has $f^{\#} \equiv 0$ and $\|f\|_p = 1$, so the conclusion fails.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Conclusion completeness / direction | The inequality is $\|f\|_{\mathcal{L}_p} \le N\|f^{\#}\|_{\mathcal{L}_p}$. The opposite direction is Theorem 3.2.4 combined with $f^{\#}\le 2\mathcal{M}f$ and carries none of the content. | ✅ `eLpNorm f (ENNReal.ofReal p) μ ≤ … * eLpNorm' (sharpFunction F f) p μ`. ❗ Predicted error: reversing the inequality, or replacing $f^{\#}$ by the maximal function $\mathcal{M}f$ (for which the bound is the elementary Theorem 3.2.4). |
| 2 | Constant dependence | $N = (2q)^pN_0^{p-1}$ with $q = p/(p-1)$ is *explicit*: it is not an unnamed constant, and $N_0$ is the regularity constant of the given filtration, not a free parameter. | ✅ `ENNReal.ofReal ((2 * (p / (p - 1))) ^ p) * F.regularityConstant ^ (p - 1)`, where `F.regularityConstant` is the `N₀` of the structure. ⚠️ An `∃ N` version depending only on `p` and `N₀` is weaker but still a reasonable formalization; a bare `∃ N` depending on `f` would be vacuous. |
| 3 | Hypothesis completeness | $\mu(\Omega) = \infty$ is required. On a probability space, $f \equiv 1$ has $f^{\#} \equiv 0$ while $\|f\|_p = 1$; the theorem is simply false there. Completeness and $\sigma$-finiteness are the chapter's standing assumptions. | ✅ `hμ : μ univ = (∞ : ℝ≥0∞)` together with `[SigmaFinite μ]` and `[μ.IsComplete]`. ❗ Predicted error: dropping `hμ`, the most consequential omission in this problem. |
| 4 | Hypothesis completeness / filtration axioms | All of Definition 3.1.1 must be carried: countable partitions of finite measure; nesting $C_n(x) \subseteq C_{n-1}(x)$; regularity $\lvert C_{n-1}(x)\rvert \le N_0\lvert C_n(x)\rvert$; and condition (i) — cells growing without bound as $n \to -\infty$ **and** the martingale-convergence property $f_{\mid n}\to f$ a.e. along a dense subclass of $\mathcal{L}_1$. Dropping (i) falsifies the theorem (see the stationary-filtration example above). | ✅ `FiltrationOfPartitions` bundles `countable_partition`, `measure_cell_ne_top`, `mem_cell`, `cell_congr`, `cell_subset`, `measure_cell_le`, `tendsto_measure_atBot`, and the pair `denseClass_dense` / `denseClass_differentiation`. ❗ Predicted error: keeping only nesting and regularity, i.e. a "dyadic-like" structure with no growth condition. |
| 5 | Faithful encoding | $f^{\#}(x) = \sup_{n<\infty}\fint_{C_n(x)}\lvert f(y) - f_{\mid n}(y)\rvert\mu(dy)$ subtracts the *conditional average function* $f_{\mid n}$ inside the integral. Since $f_{\mid n}$ is constant on $C_n(x)$ this equals $\fint_{C_n(x)}\lvert f - f_{\mid n}(x)\rvert$, but a candidate that subtracts an *arbitrary* constant $c$ and takes an infimum defines a comparable but different quantity (Exercise 3.2.5 relates them up to a factor 2). | ✅ `sharpFunction` is transcribed literally, with the inner integrand `fun y ↦ \|f y - ⨍ z in F.cell n y, f z ∂μ\|`. |
| 6 | Junk values | The supremum over $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ is unbounded in general; a real-valued `⨆` returns `0` for a set unbounded above, which would replace the right-hand side by `0` and make the theorem false. Averages over null cells need Krylov's convention $0/0 := 0$. | ✅ `sharpFunction` is `ℝ≥0∞`-valued (`⨆ n : ℤ, ENNReal.ofReal …`), so the supremum is junk-free; the averages are mathlib's `⨍ y in F.cell n x, … ∂μ`, whose `Measure.real`-normalised definition gives `0` on a null cell, matching Krylov's $0/0 := 0$. ❗ Predicted error: a real-valued sharp function. |
| 7 | Faithful encoding / the `𝓛_p` norm of `f^#` | $\|f^{\#}\|_{\mathcal{L}_p}$ must be $(\int (f^{\#})^p\,d\mu)^{1/p}$ computed for the `ℝ≥0∞`-valued $f^{\#}$; `eLpNorm` is not directly applicable to an `ℝ≥0∞`-valued function. | ✅ mathlib's `eLpNorm' g p μ = (∫⁻ x, ‖g x‖ₑ ^ p ∂μ) ^ (1/p)`, which is definitionally this for `ℝ≥0∞`-valued `g` with `ENNReal.rpow` on both sides. ❗ Predicted error: `eLpNorm (fun x ↦ (sharpFunction F f x).toReal) …`, where `toReal` sends `∞ ↦ 0` and silently truncates. |
| 8 | Hypothesis completeness | $p \in (1,\infty)$ strictly. At $p = 1$ the theorem is false (this is the classical failure of the $H^1$–BMO duality endpoint), and $q = p/(p-1)$ is undefined. | ✅ `hp : 1 < p` with `p : ℝ`. ❗ Predicted error: `1 ≤ p`, which makes `p - 1 = 0` and the constant `⊤`-valued or undefined. |
| 9 | Hypothesis completeness | $f \in \mathcal{L}_p(\Omega)$ is a hypothesis, not a conclusion: the theorem is an a priori estimate, and the extension from $\mathcal{L}_1$ to $\mathcal{L}_p$ is the content of Theorem 3.2.4. | ✅ `hf : MemLp f (ENNReal.ofReal p) μ`. ⚠️ Some sources state the sharper "if $f^{\#} \in \mathcal{L}_p$ and $f$ vanishes at infinity then $f \in \mathcal{L}_p$"; that is a strictly stronger claim than Krylov's Theorem 3.2.10 and should be flagged as out of scope. |
