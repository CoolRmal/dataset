# Criteria: krylov_sobolev_11_1_3_maximum_principle

**Statement:** [krylov_sobolev_11_1_3_maximum_principle.md](krylov_sobolev_11_1_3_maximum_principle.md) · **Lean:** [krylov_sobolev_11_1_3_maximum_principle.lean](krylov_sobolev_11_1_3_maximum_principle.lean)

A faithful formalization must bound $u$ by $\max_{\partial\Omega}u_+$ — the boundary maximum of the **positive part** — for a subsolution of a uniformly elliptic $L = a^{ij}D_{ij}+b^iD_i+c$ with $c \le 0$ on a **bounded** domain. Replacing $u_+$ by $u$ gives a false statement: on $\Omega = (-1,1)$ with $L = D^2 - 1$, the solution of $u'' = u$, $u(\pm1) = -1$ is $u = -\cosh x/\cosh 1$, so $u(0) = -1/\cosh 1 > -1 = \max_{\partial\Omega}u$. Boundedness of $\Omega$ is equally load-bearing — the whole-space analogue needs $c \le -\lambda < 0$ (Lemma 1.1.7) — and continuity up to $\bar\Omega$ is what makes the boundary maximum meaningful.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
