## Notation

Unitary means **both** $U^*U=1$ and $UU^*=1$. **Strong** continuity is continuity of $t\mapsto U(t)x$ for each $x$, not in operator norm. The generator is **unbounded**: it carries a dense domain, and self-adjointness for such an operator is two conditions, strictly stronger than symmetry. $\exp(itA)$ is defined through the spectral measure, not a power series.
