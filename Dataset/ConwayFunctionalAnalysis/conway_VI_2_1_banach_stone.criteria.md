# Criteria: conway_VI_2_1_banach_stone

**Statement:** [conway_VI_2_1_banach_stone.md](conway_VI_2_1_banach_stone.md) · **Lean:** [conway_VI_2_1_banach_stone.lean](conway_VI_2_1_banach_stone.lean)

A faithful formalization must say that a surjective *linear* isometry between the sup-norm algebras $C(X)$, $C(Y)$ of a pair of compact Hausdorff spaces is a weighted composition operator, producing simultaneously a homeomorphism $\tau : Y \to X$ and a unimodular $\alpha \in C(Y)$ satisfying the pointwise identity $(Tf)(y) = \alpha(y) f(\tau(y))$. The Hausdorff hypothesis is not decorative — without it $C(X)$ does not separate points of $X$ and no homeomorphism can be recovered. The other places to get wrong are the *direction* of $\tau$ (it goes $Y \to X$, so that $f \circ \tau$ makes sense) and the strength of "$|\alpha(y)| = 1$", which is a pointwise condition and is strictly stronger than $\|\alpha\| = 1$.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
