# Criteria: conway_VII_7_1_riesz_compact_operator_spectrum

**Statement:** [conway_VII_7_1_riesz_compact_operator_spectrum.md](conway_VII_7_1_riesz_compact_operator_spectrum.md) · **Lean:** [conway_VII_7_1_riesz_compact_operator_spectrum.lean](conway_VII_7_1_riesz_compact_operator_spectrum.lean)

A faithful formalization must present the Riesz trichotomy for the spectrum of a compact operator on an *infinite-dimensional* complex Banach space: $\sigma(A)$ is $\{0\}$, or $\{0\}$ together with finitely many nonzero eigenvalues, or $\{0\}$ together with a sequence of nonzero eigenvalues tending to $0$ — with every nonzero spectral point an eigenvalue of finite multiplicity, and with $0$ always in the spectrum. Two things are easy to get formally wrong: the phrase "$\lambda_k$ is an eigenvalue" must carry a *nonzero* eigenvector (otherwise `T x = λ • x` is satisfied by `x = 0` and the clause is vacuous), and the spectrum here is the Banach-algebra spectrum of `T : E →L[ℂ] E`, which in infinite dimensions is strictly larger than the set of eigenvalues in general.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
