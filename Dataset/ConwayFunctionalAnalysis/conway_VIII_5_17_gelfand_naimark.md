## Statement

**VIII.5.17. Theorem.** If $\mathcal{A}$ is a $C^*$-algebra, then there is a representation $(\pi, \mathcal{H})$ of $\mathcal{A}$ such that $\pi$ is an isometry. If $\mathcal{A}$ is separable, then $\mathcal{H}$ can be chosen separable.
