# Criteria: conway_II_7_6_compact_normal_spectral_theorem

**Statement:** [conway_II_7_6_compact_normal_spectral_theorem.md](conway_II_7_6_compact_normal_spectral_theorem.md) · **Lean:** [conway_II_7_6_compact_normal_spectral_theorem.lean](conway_II_7_6_compact_normal_spectral_theorem.lean)

A faithful formalization must produce, for a compact normal $T$ on a complex Hilbert space, a *countable* family of distinct nonzero eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ with the *orthogonal projections onto the corresponding eigenspaces* $P_i$, satisfying $P_iP_j = 0$ for $i \ne j$ and $T = \sum_i \lambda_i P_i$ **in the operator norm**. Two clauses do the real work and are easy to weaken accidentally: "$P_n$ is the projection of $\mathcal{H}$ onto $\ker(T-\lambda_n)$" is *two* conditions (star projection, and range exactly the eigenspace), and "converges in the metric defined by the norm on $\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{H})$" is norm convergence, not the strong operator convergence that a naive pointwise formulation would give.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
