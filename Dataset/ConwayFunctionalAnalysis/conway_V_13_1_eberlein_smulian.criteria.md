# Criteria: conway_V_13_1_eberlein_smulian

**Statement:** [conway_V_13_1_eberlein_smulian.md](conway_V_13_1_eberlein_smulian.md) · **Lean:** [conway_V_13_1_eberlein_smulian.lean](conway_V_13_1_eberlein_smulian.lean)

A faithful formalization must state a genuine three-way equivalence for an *arbitrary* subset $A$ of a Banach space, with every occurrence of "weak" interpreted in the topology $\sigma(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{X}^*)$ — i.e. in Mathlib's type synonym `WeakSpace ℂ E`, never in the norm topology of `E`. The mathematical content is concentrated in the gap between (a) and (b): in a general topological space a cluster point of a sequence need not be a subsequential limit, and Eberlein–Šmulian says that for the weak topology it is. Any encoding of "weak cluster point" that is secretly "limit of a subsequence" makes (a) ⟺ (b) trivial and destroys the theorem; likewise, computing the closure in (c) in the wrong topology yields an inequivalent statement.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis completeness | The only hypotheses are: `E` a Banach space (`NormedAddCommGroup`, `NormedSpace ℂ`, `CompleteSpace`) and `A : Set E` completely unconstrained. Completeness is genuinely needed; conversely nothing may be silently added. | ✅ `[CompleteSpace E]` present, `(A : Set E)` bare. ❗ Predicted error: adding `Bornology.IsBounded A`, `IsClosed A` or `Convex ℝ A` — all natural-looking, all absent from V.13.1, and each makes the theorem strictly weaker. |
| 2 | Conclusion completeness | All three items must appear as one `List.TFAE` of length 3, not as the single equivalence (a) ⟺ (c) plus a remark. | ✅ `List.TFAE [subsequences, clusterPoints, IsCompact (closure (toWeakSpace ℂ E '' A))]`. ❗ Predicted error: formalizing only two of the three items, most often dropping (b). |
| 3 | Faithful encoding | "Weak cluster point" in (b) must be a topological cluster point of the *sequence as a net*: `MapClusterPt (toWeakSpace ℂ E x) atTop (fun n ↦ toWeakSpace ℂ E (u n))` (i.e. `ClusterPt x (map u atTop)`). Encoding it as "some subsequence converges weakly to `x`" turns (b) into a restatement of (a) and removes the entire content of the theorem. | ✅ `MapClusterPt` used. ❗ This is the single most likely fidelity failure for this problem. |
| 4 | Faithful encoding | (c) is "the *weak* closure of $A$ is weakly compact": `closure` must be evaluated in `WeakSpace ℂ E` after transporting `A`, as in `IsCompact (closure (toWeakSpace ℂ E '' A))`. Writing `toWeakSpace ℂ E '' closure A` (norm closure, then transported) names a strictly smaller set in general and gives an inequivalent statement; `IsCompact A` in `E` states norm compactness and is plainly wrong. | ✅ Closure and compactness are both taken in `WeakSpace ℂ E`. ❗ Predicted error: norm closure, or `IsCompact A`. |
| 5 | Mathlib conventions | The weak topology on `E` is `WeakSpace ℂ E` with the transport map `toWeakSpace ℂ E : E ≃ₗ[ℂ] WeakSpace ℂ E`, *not* `WeakDual ℂ E` (which is the weak-\* topology on the dual). Stating (a)/(b) instead by testing against functionals, `∀ φ : E →L[ℂ] ℂ, Tendsto (fun n ↦ φ (u (φ' n))) atTop (𝓝 (φ x))`, is equivalent and acceptable — but a candidate that does so usually cannot state (c) and reverts to a norm-topology claim. | ✅ Uses `toWeakSpace`/`WeakSpace` uniformly across all three items. ❗ Predicted error: `WeakDual ℂ E` confusion. |
| 6 | Semantic closeness | (a) must give a *strictly monotone* reindexing and a limit that is allowed to lie anywhere in $\mathcal{X}$ (not necessarily in $A$ or $\overline{A}$): `∃ φ : ℕ → ℕ, StrictMono φ ∧ ∃ x : E, Tendsto …`. Demanding `x ∈ A` would falsify (a) for non-weakly-closed `A`; dropping `StrictMono` would not give a subsequence. | ✅ Both present, `x : E` unconstrained. |
| 7 | Quantifier structure | Sequences "of elements of $A$" are encoded as `u : ℕ → E` together with `∀ n, u n ∈ A`, and the whole of (a)/(b) is a `∀`-statement over such sequences. Using `u : ℕ → A` (subtype) is equivalent; using `∃ u` anywhere inverts the statement. | ✅ `∀ u : ℕ → E, (∀ n, u n ∈ A) → …` in both (a) and (b). |
| 8 | Scalars | Conway's $\mathcal{X}$ is over $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$; the Lean fixes `NormedSpace ℂ E`. No content is lost, since $\sigma(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{X}^*)$ for a complex space coincides with the weak topology of its underlying real space. | ⚠️ Acceptable; an `RCLike 𝕜`-parametrized version would cover the text exactly and should not be penalized in a candidate. |
