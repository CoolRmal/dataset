## Notation

$\partial'Q$ is the **parabolic** boundary: $\partial Q$ minus the points reachable from $Q$ only from earlier times. For a cylinder it is the bottom and the lateral surface — the **top is excluded**. $L$ acts in the space variables only. The exponent pairs $(\delta/2,\delta)$ and $(1+\delta/2,2+\delta)$ are locked by the parabolic scaling.
