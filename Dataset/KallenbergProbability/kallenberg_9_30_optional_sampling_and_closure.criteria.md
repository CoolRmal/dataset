# Criteria: kallenberg_9_30_optional_sampling_and_closure

**Statement:** [kallenberg_9_30_optional_sampling_and_closure.md](kallenberg_9_30_optional_sampling_and_closure.md) · **Lean:** [kallenberg_9_30_optional_sampling_and_closure.lean](kallenberg_9_30_optional_sampling_and_closure.lean)

A faithful formalization needs three pieces: integrability of $X_\tau$ for a bounded optional time, the optional sampling *inequality* $X_{\sigma\wedge\tau} \le \mathbb{E}(X_\tau \mid \mathcal{F}_\sigma)$ a.s. with $\mathcal{F}_\sigma$ the stopping-time $\sigma$-algebra (not $\mathcal{F}_t$ for a fixed $t$), and the closure clause as a genuine `↔` with uniform integrability of $X^+$. The mathematical hazard sits in the closure clause: an unbounded optional time may take the value $+\infty$, and mathlib's `stoppedValue u τ ω = u (τ ω).untopA ω` uses `WithTop.untopA = untopD (Classical.arbitrary _)`, so on $\{\tau = \infty\}$ the "stopped value" is $X$ evaluated at an *arbitrary fixed time*, not the a.s. limit $X_\infty = \lim_{t\to\infty}X_t$ that Kallenberg's closure statement intends.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis completeness | Both right-continuity assumptions of the text are needed: right-continuity of the filtration and of the paths of $X$. Neither may be dropped, and the process must be a submartingale on $\mathbb{R}_+$ with a continuous time index. | ✅ `[ℱ.IsRightContinuous]` on `ℱ : Filtration ℝ≥0 ‹MeasurableSpace Ω›`, and `hXright : ∀ᵐ ω ∂μ, ∀ t, ContinuousWithinAt (fun s ↦ X s ω) (Ici t) t`; `hX : Submartingale X ℱ μ` (which bundles adaptedness, integrability and the conditional inequality). ❗ Trap: formalizing the discrete-time optional sampling theorem (`Filtration ℕ`), where both right-continuity hypotheses evaporate. |
| 2 | Faithful encoding | $\mathcal{F}_\sigma$ must be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the optional time $\sigma$, i.e. `hσ.measurableSpace` (`IsStoppingTime.measurableSpace`), and the conclusion an a.e. **inequality**. | ✅ `stoppedValue X (fun ω ↦ min (σ ω) (τ ω)) ≤ᵐ[μ] μ[stoppedValue X τ \| hσ.measurableSpace]`. ❗ Trap: conditioning on `ℱ i` for a fixed `i`, or stating equality (that is the martingale case, false for submartingales). |
| 3 | Conclusion completeness | Both conclusions of the bounded part: `Integrable (stoppedValue X τ) μ` **and** the sampling inequality. The integrability claim is what makes the conditional expectation meaningful (`condExp` is `0` for non-integrable inputs). | ✅ Both present as conjuncts. ❗ Trap: dropping the integrability claim and relying on `condExp` silently. |
| 4 | Junk values | `stoppedValue X τ` is `X (τ ω).untopA ω`, with `untopA` returning `Classical.arbitrary ℝ≥0` on `⊤`. For the bounded time `τ` this only affects a `μ`-null set (`hτbounded`), so the first two conclusions are sound; but in the closure clause `τ'` ranges over *all* stopping times, so on `{τ' = ∞}` the statement compares junk values rather than $X_\infty$. | ⚠️/❗ This is the statement's real weak point: Kallenberg's extension to unbounded $\tau$ presupposes $X_\infty = \lim_{t\to\infty}X_t$ (which exists a.s. exactly under the uniform-integrability hypothesis) and defines $X_\tau := X_\infty$ on $\{\tau=\infty\}$. A stronger formalization would extend `X` to `WithTop ℝ≥0` by that limit before applying `stoppedValue`. |
| 5 | Conclusion completeness | The closure clause is an `↔`, and its right-hand side must quantify over *arbitrary* (in particular unbounded) optional times `τ'` and, for each, over arbitrary `σ'`, asserting both integrability and the sampling inequality. | ✅ `UniformIntegrable (fun t ω ↦ max (X t ω) 0) 1 μ ↔ ∀ τ', IsStoppingTime ℱ τ' → Integrable (stoppedValue X τ') μ ∧ ∀ σ', ∀ hσ' : IsStoppingTime ℱ σ', stoppedValue X (fun ω ↦ min (σ' ω) (τ' ω)) ≤ᵐ[μ] μ[stoppedValue X τ' \| hσ'.measurableSpace]`. ❗ Trap: a one-directional implication (UI ⇒ extension), or re-using the bounded `τ` and thereby making the clause vacuous. |
| 6 | Faithful encoding | "$X^+$ is uniformly integrable" is uniform integrability of the family `fun t ω ↦ max (X t ω) 0` indexed by the *time* `t`, at exponent `1`. | ✅ `UniformIntegrable (fun t ω ↦ max (X t ω) 0) 1 μ`. ⚠️ Mathlib's `UniformIntegrable` additionally bundles `AEStronglyMeasurable` and a uniform $L^1$ bound; both hold automatically for a submartingale, so this matches the text. ❗ Trap: uniform integrability of `X` itself rather than of its positive part, or of the *stopped* family. |
| 7 | Faithful encoding | "$\tau$ bounded" should be a uniform bound by a real time. The ground truth uses an a.s. bound. | ⚠️ `hτbounded : ∃ u : ℝ≥0, ∀ᵐ ω ∂μ, τ ω ≤ u` is an a.s. bound where the text says "bounded"; this makes the theorem formally stronger and is harmless, but `∀ ω, τ ω ≤ u` is the literal reading. |
| 8 | Mathlib conventions | Optional times are `Ω → WithTop ℝ≥0` with `IsStoppingTime ℱ`, matching mathlib's current signature; $\sigma \wedge \tau$ is `min`; conditional expectation uses the `μ[f \| m]` notation; a.e. relations use `≤ᵐ[μ]`; the index `ℝ≥0` models $\mathbb{R}_+$. | ✅ All idiomatic. Note `IsStoppingTime.measurableSpace ≤ ‹MeasurableSpace Ω›` holds in mathlib, so `condExp` is not silently `0` for that reason. |
