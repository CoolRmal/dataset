## Notation

Kallenberg's "local" properties are defined by **localization**: there are optional times $\tau_n\uparrow\infty$ such that each stopped, $X_0$-centred process has the property. A local martingale is therefore not a martingale.

**Predictable** is strictly stronger than adapted, and it is what makes the decomposition unique. Kallenberg's *increasing process* bundles right-continuity, adaptedness and $A_0=0$.
