# Criteria: kallenberg_23_6_functional_central_limit

**Statement:** [kallenberg_23_6_functional_central_limit.md](kallenberg_23_6_functional_central_limit.md) · **Lean:** [kallenberg_23_6_functional_central_limit.lean](kallenberg_23_6_functional_central_limit.lean)

Donsker's theorem is a statement about weak convergence **on path space**: the polygonal partial-sum processes $X^n$, viewed as random elements of $C(\mathbb{R}_+, \mathbb{R}^d)$, converge in distribution to a $d$-dimensional Brownian motion. A formalization that only asserts convergence of the finite-dimensional distributions states a strictly weaker fact (it omits tightness, which is the entire difficulty), and one that drops the linear-interpolation term $(nt - [nt])\xi_{[nt]+1}$ formalizes the càdlàg/Skorokhod variant instead, whose paths do not lie in $C$. On the hypothesis side the sensitive points are mutual (not pairwise) independence of the $\xi_k$, the *full* covariance condition $\delta_{ij}$ (including the off-diagonal, i.e. uncorrelated coordinates), and an $L^2$ assumption without which the Bochner integrals expressing the mean and covariance silently evaluate to the junk value `0`.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis completeness | "i.i.d." must be *mutual* independence of the whole sequence plus equality of laws. | ✅ `hiid : iIndepFun ξ μ ∧ ∀ n, IdentDistrib (ξ n) (ξ 0) μ μ`. ❗ Trap: pairwise `IndepFun` (insufficient), or independence without `IdentDistrib`, or `IdentDistrib` without independence. |
| 2 | Hypothesis completeness / junk values | Mean $0$ and covariance $\delta_{ij}$ must both be stated, and both are Bochner integrals that return `0` as junk for non-integrable integrands — the $L^2$ hypothesis is what makes them meaningful (and makes `ξ 0 i * ξ 0 j` integrable by Cauchy–Schwarz). | ✅ `hsecondMoment : ∀ i, MemLp (fun ω ↦ ξ 0 ω i) 2 μ`, `hmean : ∀ i, ∫ ω, ξ 0 ω i ∂μ = 0`, `hcovariance : ∀ i j, ∫ ω, ξ 0 ω i * ξ 0 ω j ∂μ = if i = j then 1 else 0`. ❗ Trap: stating only `variance = 1` per coordinate and omitting the off-diagonal vanishing, which no longer pins the limit to a *standard* Brownian motion. |
| 3 | Faithful encoding | The processes must be the polygonally interpolated partial sums, with the correct summation range $k \le nt$ and the interpolation term. | ✅ `hX : ∀ n, 0 < n → ∀ ω t i, X n ω t i = (Real.sqrt (n : ℝ))⁻¹ * ((∑ k ∈ Finset.Icc 1 ⌊(n : ℝ) * (t : ℝ)⌋₊, ξ k ω i) + ((n : ℝ) * (t : ℝ) - ⌊(n : ℝ) * (t : ℝ)⌋₊) * ξ (⌊(n : ℝ) * (t : ℝ)⌋₊ + 1) ω i)`; `Finset.Icc 1 ⌊nt⌋₊ = {1, …, [nt]}` matches $\sum_{k \le nt}$ for a sequence indexed from `1`. ❗ Trap: dropping the interpolation term (the D-space version), or using `Finset.range ⌊nt⌋₊` (off by one). |
| 4 | Faithful encoding | The convergence must take place in $C_{\mathbb{R}_+,\mathbb{R}^d}$: the random elements are valued in `C(ℝ≥0, Fin d → ℝ)` carrying its Borel $\sigma$-algebra for the topology of locally uniform convergence. | ✅ `X : ℕ → Ω → C(ℝ≥0, Fin d → ℝ)`, with `[MeasurableSpace C(ℝ≥0, Fin d → ℝ)] [BorelSpace C(ℝ≥0, Fin d → ℝ)]` pinning the $\sigma$-algebra to the Borel one. ❗ Trap: stating convergence of the finite-dimensional marginals only, or working on `C([0,1], ℝ^d)` / `ℝ≥0 → Fin d → ℝ` (where the product $\sigma$-algebra makes the theorem false or vacuous). |
| 5 | Convergence mode | The conclusion is convergence **in distribution** of path-space-valued random elements defined on possibly different probability spaces. | ✅ `TendstoInDistribution X atTop B (fun _ ↦ μ) μ'`, with the `X n` on `(Ω, μ)` and `B` on `(Ω', μ')`. ❗ Trap: a.s. convergence or convergence in probability (impossible across different spaces), or `Tendsto` of the mapped measures in `Measure _` rather than in `ProbabilityMeasure _`. |
| 6 | Faithful encoding | The limit must be a genuine $d$-dimensional Brownian motion: each coordinate a real Brownian motion **and** the coordinates independent. | ✅ `hB : IsBrownianVector B μ'`, i.e. `(∀ i, IsBrownianReal (fun t ω ↦ B ω t i) μ') ∧ ∀ times : Finset ℝ≥0, iIndepFun (fun i ω (t : times) ↦ B ω t i) μ'`. ❗ Trap: asserting only that each coordinate is Brownian and forgetting independence, which does not determine the law of `B`. |
| 7 | Semantic closeness | Independence of the coordinate processes is encoded through all finite-dimensional restrictions rather than directly on the path space. | ⚠️ Equivalent (finite-dimensional cylinder sets form a generating $\pi$-system), but `iIndepFun (fun i ω ↦ fun t ↦ B ω t i) μ'` on the path/function space would be a more direct rendering of "$B^1,\dots,B^d$ are independent". |
| 8 | Semantic closeness | `X` is a parameter constrained by an equation rather than a defined object, and the constraint is only imposed for `0 < n`. | ⚠️ Acceptable — the conclusion is an `atTop` limit, so the unconstrained `X 0` is irrelevant, and `Real.sqrt 0⁻¹` would be junk anyway — but a candidate must not leave `X n` unconstrained for infinitely many `n`. `hXmeas : ∀ n, AEMeasurable (X n) μ` is genuinely needed (the path space is not discrete); `hξmeas` is largely redundant given `hsecondMoment` and `IdentDistrib`. |
