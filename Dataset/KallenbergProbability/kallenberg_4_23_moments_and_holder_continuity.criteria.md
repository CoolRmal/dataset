# Criteria: kallenberg_4_23_moments_and_holder_continuity

**Statement:** [kallenberg_4_23_moments_and_holder_continuity.md](kallenberg_4_23_moments_and_holder_continuity.md) · **Lean:** [kallenberg_4_23_moments_and_holder_continuity.lean](kallenberg_4_23_moments_and_holder_continuity.lean)

The whole content of the Kolmogorov–Loève–Chentsov theorem is that a *version* (modification) of $X$ has Hölder paths — $X$ itself need not have a single continuous path, since the moment bound is insensitive to modification on null sets. A formalization that concludes anything about `X` rather than about a newly produced process `Y` with `∀ t, X t =ᵐ[μ] Y t` is vacuously wrong. The second sensitive point is the moment hypothesis $\mathbb{E}\rho(X_s,X_t)^a \lesssim |s-t|^{d+b}$: the exponent must be $d + b$ with $d$ the *dimension of the index space*, the constant must be uniform in $s,t$ ($\exists C\,\forall s\,t$, not $\forall s\,t\,\exists C$), and the expectation must be a lower Lebesgue integral, since a Bochner `∫` would evaluate to `0` exactly in the cases the hypothesis is meant to exclude.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
