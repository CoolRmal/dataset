## Statement

**Theorem 23.2 (tightness and relative compactness, Prohorov).** For a set $\Xi$ of random elements in a metric space $S$, we have (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) with

- **(i)** $\Xi$ is tight,
- **(ii)** $\Xi$ is relatively compact in distribution,

and equivalence holds when $S$ is separable and complete.
