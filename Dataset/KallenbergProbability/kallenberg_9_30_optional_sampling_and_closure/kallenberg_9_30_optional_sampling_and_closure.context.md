## Notation

Optional times may take the value $+\infty$, which is why the closure half needs a terminal $X_\infty$. $\mathcal{F}_\sigma$ is the stopped $\sigma$-algebra. For a submartingale the sampling relation is an **inequality**, and the closure clause is an **equivalence**. $X^+=\max(X,0)$.
