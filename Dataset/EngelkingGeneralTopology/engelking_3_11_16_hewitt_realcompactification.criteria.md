# Criteria: engelking_3_11_16_hewitt_realcompactification

**Statement:** [engelking_3_11_16_hewitt_realcompactification.md](engelking_3_11_16_hewitt_realcompactification.md) · **Lean:** [engelking_3_11_16_hewitt_realcompactification.lean](engelking_3_11_16_hewitt_realcompactification.lean)

A faithful formalization must (a) transcribe Engelking's *negative* definition of realcompactness — a Tychonoff space admitting no **proper** dense Tychonoff extension to which all real-valued continuous functions extend — and (b) assert both existence and uniqueness of $\nu X$: existence of a realcompact $\nu X$ with a dense embedding $\nu$, the real-function extension property (ii), the universal property (iii) for maps into arbitrary realcompact targets, and uniqueness *up to a homeomorphism compatible with the embeddings*. The difficulty is concentrated in the auxiliary definition: `IsRealcompact` is a negated existential over spaces, and dropping any single conjunct inside it silently turns it into a predicate satisfied by nothing (or by everything). Mathlib has no realcompactness or Hewitt realcompactification, so everything here is hand-rolled and must be checked literally.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Faithful encoding (auxiliary definition) | In `IsRealcompact`, (RC1) is the *chain* $r(X) \neq \overline{r(X)} = \tilde X$: both `range r ≠ closure (range r)` (the extension is **proper**) and `closure (range r) = univ` (it is **dense**) are needed. Dropping the properness clause is fatal, not cosmetic: with `r = id` one always gets a dense Tychonoff "extension" to which every $f$ extends, so `IsRealcompact` would be satisfied by *no* space and the theorem would become unprovable. | ✅ `range r ≠ closure (range r) ∧ closure (range r) = univ` are both present, together with `T35Space` on the extension and the extension property (RC2). ❗ Predicted error: candidates keeping only density. |
| 2 | Separation axioms (the Engelking convention) | Engelking's "Tychonoff" = completely regular **+ $T_1$**, i.e. mathlib's `T35Space` (`T0Space` + `CompletelyRegularSpace`), not bare `CompletelyRegularSpace`. This matters: mathlib's `CompletelyRegularSpace` holds for indiscrete spaces, into which no embedding of a $T_0$ space exists, so with that weakening the existence claim `∃ Y, … IsEmbedding ν …` becomes false. | ✅ `[T35Space X]` on the hypothesis and `T35Space` inside `IsRealcompact`. ❗ Predicted error: `[CompletelyRegularSpace X]` or `[RegularSpace X]` in place of `[T35Space X]`. |
| 3 | Conclusion completeness | All four assertions of the theorem must appear as conjuncts under a single `∃ Y ν`: (i) embedding with dense range, (ii) extension of every continuous $f \colon X \to \mathbb{R}$, (iii) extension of every continuous map into a realcompact space, and uniqueness. Item (iii) is stated by Engelking as an additional property of the *same* $\nu X$ and is the part most often dropped. | ✅ One `∃ (Y) (tY) (ν)` bundling `IsRealcompact Y`, `IsEmbedding ν`, `closure (range ν) = univ`, (ii), (iii) and the uniqueness clause. |
| 4 | Semantic closeness (uniqueness) | "Exactly one up to a homeomorphism" must be the *rigid* uniqueness: any $Z$ that is realcompact and satisfies (i)+(ii) admits a homeomorphism $h \colon Y \to Z$ **with $h \circ \nu = \zeta$**. A bare `Nonempty (Y ≃ₜ Z)` is strictly weaker (it forgets that the two copies of $X$ correspond) and is the standard degradation of this clause. Equally important: the uniqueness hypothesis must be (i)+(ii) only — assuming (iii) of $Z$ as well would make uniqueness a much cheaper statement. | ✅ `∃ h : Homeomorph Y Z, h ∘ ν = ζ`, with hypotheses on `Z` exactly `IsRealcompact`, `IsEmbedding ζ`, dense range, and the real-extension property. |
| 5 | Semantic closeness (universal property vs. mere existence) | Clause (iii) must quantify over *all* realcompact targets and produce a continuous extension, i.e. a genuine universal property, not just "there is a continuous surjection/retraction". `g ∘ ν = f` as an equality of functions is the faithful reading of $f^{\nu}\nu = f$. | ✅ `∀ Z tZ, IsRealcompact Z → ∀ f, Continuous f → ∃ g, Continuous g ∧ g ∘ ν = f`. ⚠️ Uniqueness of $f^{\nu}$ (automatic from density) is not asserted; harmless, since Engelking does not assert it either. |
| 6 | Universe hygiene | `IsRealcompact` quantifies the hypothetical extension over `Type (u+1)` and clause (iii) quantifies targets over `Type u`. Neither is literally "all Tychonoff spaces". | ⚠️ Acceptable but improvable. A Tychonoff space containing a dense copy of `X` has cardinality at most $2^{2^{\lvert X\rvert }}$, so it can be transported into `Type u`, and `Type (u+1)` contains `ULift`s of all `Type u` spaces; the definition is therefore equivalent to the intended one, but the argument is non-trivial. Clause (iii) restricted to `Type u` targets is a genuine (mild) weakening: a `Type v`-polymorphic version would be stronger. |
| 7 | Mathlib conventions | Mathlib has no realcompactness, no Hewitt realcompactification and no `Realcompactification` API (checked: nothing under `Mathlib/Topology`), so a custom predicate is required; but the *available* pieces must be reused: `Topology.IsEmbedding`, `Homeomorph`, `Continuous`. `closure (range ν) = univ` is `DenseRange ν` spelled out — `DenseRange` would be more idiomatic and is the form to accept from candidates. | ✅ / ⚠️ Correct notions, but `DenseRange ν` (and `letI`/instance binders instead of the `@…tY…` applications forced by binding `tY : TopologicalSpace Y` as an ordinary variable) would read better. |
| 8 | Faithful encoding (which compactification) | $\nu X$ is the **Hewitt realcompactification**, not the Čech–Stone compactification: a candidate that instantiates the existential with `StoneCech X`, or asserts $\nu X$ is compact, states a false theorem ($\beta X$ is realcompact, but $\beta \mathbb{N} \neq \nu \mathbb{N}$ since $\mathbb{N}$ is already realcompact). Realcompactness of the produced space must be asserted, not compactness. | ✅ Only `IsRealcompact Y` is asserted; no compactness is smuggled in. ❗ Predicted error: conflating $\nu X$ with $\beta X$. |
