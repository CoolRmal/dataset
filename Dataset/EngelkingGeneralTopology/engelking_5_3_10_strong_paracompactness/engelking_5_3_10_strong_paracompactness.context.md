## Notation

Engelking builds separation axioms into his names: *regular* and *normal* include $T_1$, *compact* and *paracompact* include Hausdorff, and *Tychonoff* is completely regular plus $T_1$. Modern usage keeps these apart, so each must be restored explicitly.

**Star-finite** means each member meets only finitely many members — a condition on the family, unrelated to local finiteness. The four items differ in three independent ways: open versus closed refinement, star-finite versus star-countable, and local finiteness present or absent.
