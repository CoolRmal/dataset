# Criteria: engelking_5_3_10_strong_paracompactness

**Statement:** [engelking_5_3_10_strong_paracompactness.md](engelking_5_3_10_strong_paracompactness.md) · **Lean:** [engelking_5_3_10_strong_paracompactness.lean](engelking_5_3_10_strong_paracompactness.lean)

A faithful formalization must state a four-way equivalence in which each item is a *cover-refinement* property with a precise adjectival load — star-finite open, closed locally finite star-finite, closed locally finite star-countable, star-countable open — over a space that is regular in Engelking's sense, i.e. regular **and** $T_1$. The specific difficulty is bookkeeping: three independent adjectives (closed/open, locally finite, star-finite/star-countable) attach to the refinement in items (ii)–(iv), and each of them must land on the refinement rather than on the original cover; on top of that, the underlying notion of strong paracompactness carries Engelking's built-in Hausdorff requirement, which mathlib's paracompactness vocabulary never supplies.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Separation axioms (the Engelking convention) | Engelking's "regular space" includes $T_1$; mathlib's `RegularSpace` does not. The omission is not cosmetic here: for the two-point indiscrete space (which is `RegularSpace` in mathlib) the only open cover is essentially $\{X\}$, so (ii), (iii) and (iv) hold trivially, while (i) fails because `IsStronglyParacompact` demands `T2Space X`. Without `[T1Space X]` the `List.TFAE` is therefore false. | ✅ Both `[RegularSpace X]` and `[T1Space X]` are assumed, which together are exactly Engelking's regularity. ❗ Predicted error: `[RegularSpace X]` alone, or `[T3Space X]` (equivalent here, and acceptable), or promoting regularity into one of the TFAE items. |
| 2 | Faithful encoding (auxiliary definition) | `IsStronglyParacompact` must be "Hausdorff **and** every open cover has a star-finite open refinement" — Engelking's Hausdorff conjunct is part of the definition, and its refinement must be an open *cover* that refines and is star-finite. | ✅ `T2Space X ∧ ∀ ι U, IsOpenCover U → ∃ κ V, IsOpenCover V ∧ Refines V U ∧ IsStarFiniteFamily V`. Note the `T2Space` conjunct is redundant given `[RegularSpace X] [T1Space X]`, but it keeps the definition faithful when reused elsewhere. |
| 3 | Faithful encoding (star-finite / star-countable) | The definitions must be Engelking's: for every $s_0$, $\{s : A_s \cap A_{s_0} \neq \emptyset\}$ is finite (resp. countable). Frequent corruptions: "each point lies in only finitely many members" (that is point-finiteness, strictly weaker than star-finiteness), or "the family itself is finite/countable" (strictly stronger). | ✅ `IsStarFiniteFamily A := ∀ i, {j \| (A i ∩ A j).Nonempty}.Finite` and the `Countable` analogue, matching the text verbatim. ⚠️ These count *indices*, not distinct sets; for the existential refinements in (i)–(iv) this is harmless (re-index injectively), and it is the stronger reading. |
| 4 | Conclusion completeness | All four items in Engelking's order, with the correct open/closed alternation: (ii) and (iii) ask for a **closed** refinement that is locally finite and star-finite / star-countable; (iv) asks for a **star-countable open** refinement. Dropping `LocallyFinite` from (ii)/(iii), or making (ii)–(iv) all open, collapses the theorem. | ✅ `closedLocallyFiniteStarFinite`, `closedLocallyFiniteStarCountable`, `starCountableOpen` are exactly these; the `List.TFAE` lists them after `IsStronglyParacompact X` in Engelking's order. |
| 5 | Faithful encoding (closed cover) | In (ii) and (iii), "closed refinement" means a *cover* of $X$ by closed sets that refines the given cover — `IsClosedCover F` (`(∀ i, IsClosed (F i)) ∧ ⋃ i, F i = univ`) plus `Refines F U`. A candidate that only requires the members to be closed subsets of members of $U$, without requiring that they cover $X$, states something vacuous. | ✅ `IsClosedCover F ∧ Refines F U ∧ …` in both items. |
| 6 | Semantic closeness (quantifier structure) | Each item is `∀ (open cover) → ∃ (refinement)`; the refinement's index type must be existentially quantified (a refinement generally cannot be indexed by the original index type — that would be a *precise* refinement and is a different, stronger statement). | ✅ `∀ ι U, IsOpenCover U → ∃ κ F, …` in all three items, with `Refines` in the correct direction (`∀ j, ∃ i, F j ⊆ U i`). |
| 7 | Mathlib conventions / universes | Mathlib has no strong paracompactness, star-finiteness or star-countability, so the custom definitions are necessary; `LocallyFinite`, `IsOpen`, `IsClosed`, `List.TFAE` are the right mathlib pieces, and `List.TFAE` is the idiomatic packaging of a four-way equivalence. All cover index types live in a single free universe `v`. | ✅ / ⚠️ Conventions are right. The free universe `v` (shared between `ι` and `κ`, and separately auto-bound inside `IsStronglyParacompact`) is noise: fixing everything at `Type u` — every cover can be re-indexed by a subfamily of `Set X` — would make the four items line up exactly. |
