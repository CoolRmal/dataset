## Statement

**5.1.38. The Tamano Theorem.** For every Tychonoff space $X$ the following conditions are equivalent:

1. **(i)** the space $X$ is paracompact;
2. **(ii)** for every compactification $cX$ of the space $X$ the Cartesian product $X \times cX$ is normal;
3. **(iii)** the Cartesian product $X \times \beta X$ is normal;
4. **(iv)** there exists a compactification $cX$ of $X$ such that $X \times cX$ is normal.
