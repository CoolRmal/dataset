## Statement

**5.1.9. Theorem.** For every $T_1$-space $X$ the following conditions are equivalent:

1. **(i)** the space $X$ is paracompact;
2. **(ii)** every open cover of the space $X$ has a locally finite partition of unity subordinated to it;
3. **(iii)** every open cover of $X$ has a partition of unity subordinated to it.
