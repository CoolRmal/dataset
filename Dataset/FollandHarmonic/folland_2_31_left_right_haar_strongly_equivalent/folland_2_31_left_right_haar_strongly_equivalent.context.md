## Notation

**Strongly equivalent** is Folland's Proposition 2.23 condition — a *continuous, strictly positive*
density against compactly supported test functions — and is strictly stronger than mutual absolute
continuity. $\rho$ is not an arbitrary right Haar measure but the specific one $\rho(E) = \lambda(E^{-1})$.
$\Delta$ is fixed by $\lambda(Ex) = \Delta(x)\lambda(E)$; the opposite convention, in which the pushforward
along right translation is scaled, gives the reciprocal, so $\Delta(x^{-1})$ here is $\Delta'(x)$ there.
