## Notation

$\Delta$ is Folland's modular function, fixed by $\lambda(Ex) = \Delta(x)\lambda(E)$ for a **left** Haar measure. The opposite convention, in which the pushforward along right translation is scaled, gives the reciprocal, so the weight $\Delta(x^{-1})$ here is $\Delta'(x)$ there. Whichever is used, the weight must be the reciprocal of the factor by which right translation by $x$ scales $\lambda$.
