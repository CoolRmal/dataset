## Notation

$\Phi$ is not an arbitrary isomorphism: (4.29) pins it down as **evaluation**, $\langle\Phi(x),\xi\rangle
= \langle x,\xi\rangle$. Merely asserting that $G$ and $\widehat{\widehat{G}}$ are isomorphic is much
weaker and misses the theorem. "Isomorphism of topological groups" is a group isomorphism that is also a
homeomorphism — a continuous bijective homomorphism is not enough, since its inverse need not be
continuous.
