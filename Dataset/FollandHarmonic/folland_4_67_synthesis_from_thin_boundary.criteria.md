# Criteria: folland_4_67_synthesis_from_thin_boundary

**Statement:** [folland_4_67_synthesis_from_thin_boundary.md](folland_4_67_synthesis_from_thin_boundary.md) · **Lean:** [folland_4_67_synthesis_from_thin_boundary.lean](folland_4_67_synthesis_from_thin_boundary.lean)

A delicate positive synthesis criterion: if the boundaries of $\nu(I)$ and $\nu(f)$ meet in a set with no nonempty perfect subset (in particular, a countable set), then $f$ lies in $I$. The two easy ways to get it wrong are to forget the inclusion $\nu(f) \supset \nu(I)$ — without which the conclusion is hopeless — and to weaken "contains no nonempty perfect set" to "is countable" or "is empty", both of which give strictly weaker theorems.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.

| # | Category | Criterion / potential error | Assessment of ground truth |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis completeness | $\nu(f) \supset \nu(I)$ is required; it is the elementary necessary condition for $f \in I$. | ✅ `hspec : hull μ I ⊆ hull μ {f}`. ❗ Predicted error: omitting it. |
| 2 | Faithful encoding | The thinness condition is "contains no nonempty perfect set", i.e. `∀ P, P.Nonempty → Perfect P → ¬ P ⊆ ∂ν(I) ∩ ∂ν(f)`. Countability implies it but is strictly stronger. | ✅ Stated with mathlib's `Perfect`. ❗ Predicted error: `Set.Countable (∂ν(I) ∩ ∂ν(f))`, a weaker theorem. |
| 3 | Faithful encoding | `∂` is the topological boundary in `Ĝ`, taken of each hull separately and then intersected. | ✅ `frontier (hull μ I) ∩ frontier (hull μ {f})`. ❗ Predicted error: `frontier (hull μ I ∩ hull μ {f})`. |
| 4 | Hypothesis completeness | `I` must be a closed ideal and `f` an `𝓛¹` function. | ✅ the `Submodule` structure, `hclosed`, `hideal`, `hf`. Because mathlib has no ready-made `𝓛¹(G)` Banach algebra for a general locally compact group, the statement works with sets of *functions* together with `Submodule ℂ (G → ℂ)` and `IsLpClosed 1 μ` in place of a closed subspace of the `Lp` quotient. This is faithful — the `Lp` quotient of an `𝓛¹`-closed subspace of functions is a closed subspace and conversely — but a candidate must supply both the linearity and the `𝓛¹`-closedness, since either alone is strictly weaker. |
| 5 | Semantic closeness | `ν(f)` abbreviates `ν({f})`, the hull of the singleton ideal generated by `f`. | ✅ `hull μ {f}`. |
