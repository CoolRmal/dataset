## Notation

Continuity of a unitary representation is in the **strong** operator topology — $x \mapsto \pi(x)v$ is
continuous for each fixed $v$ — not in the norm topology, which would be far too strong. **Irreducible**
refers to *closed* invariant subspaces only; every representation has plenty of non-closed invariant
subspaces. The Hilbert space is required to be nonzero, or the condition is vacuous. Each pair of points
may need its own representation.
