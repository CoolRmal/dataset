# Criteria: grafakos_4_3_15_carleson_hunt_line

**Statement:** [grafakos_4_3_15_carleson_hunt_line.md](grafakos_4_3_15_carleson_hunt_line.md) · **Lean:** [grafakos_4_3_15_carleson_hunt_line.lean](grafakos_4_3_15_carleson_hunt_line.lean)

Theorem 4.3.15 is the *maximal-operator* form of Carleson–Hunt: an $L^p$ bound, $1<p<\infty$, for $\mathcal{C}^{**}(f)(x)=\sup_{R>0}\big|\int_{|\xi|\le R}\widehat f(\xi)e^{2\pi i x\xi}d\xi\big|$ on a dense test class. The two things to get right are that the object bounded is a **supremum over the truncation parameter** (from which a.e. convergence of the partial Fourier integrals is *derived*, and which must not be replaced by a bare pointwise `lim` — that limit is exactly what is not known to exist before the theorem is proved), and that the supremum is taken in a complete lattice so that it is never a junk value. The constant must be uniform in $f$ but is allowed to depend on $p$.

Legend: ✅ ground truth satisfies the criterion · ⚠️ ground truth acceptable but improvable · ❗ trap — known/likely model error to check in candidate statements.
