## Statement

**Proposition 2.2.16.** (Hausdorff–Young inequality) For every function $f$ in $L^p(\mathbb{R}^n)$ we have the estimate
$$\|\widehat{f}\|_{p'} \le \|f\|_p$$
whenever $1 \le p \le 2$.
